AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Program

Week 5: Business Analytics Case Study For Batch A(1st of May to 1st of July)

Dear Intern,

Welcome to Week 5 of the AnalystLab Africa Internship Program.

This week, you will apply the analytical skills you have developed in previous weeks to solve a real-world business problem using data. Your goal is not only to analyze data but also to provide business insights and recommendations that can support decision-making.

Learning Objective

By the end of this task, you should be able to:

- Understand a business problem from a data perspective.
- Analyze business data to uncover trends and patterns.
- Identify factors influencing business outcomes.
- Communicate findings effectively to stakeholders.
- Make data-driven business recommendations.

Dataset Options

Choose one of the following datasets:

Option 1: Bank Marketing Dataset

"https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/janiobachmann/bank-marketing-dataset" (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/janiobachmann/bank-marketing-dataset)

Option 2: Telco Customer Churn Dataset

"https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn" (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn)


Task Instructions

Step 1: Understand the Business Problem

Before analyzing the data, identify the business challenge represented by the dataset.


Bank Marketing Dataset

- Why do some customers subscribe to term deposits while others do not?
- Which customer groups are more likely to respond positively to marketing campaigns?

Telco Customer Churn Dataset

- Why are customers leaving the company?
- Which factors contribute most to customer churn?

Clearly define the business problem you intend to solve.


Step 2: Perform Data Exploration and Analysis

Conduct an exploratory analysis of the dataset.

Your analysis should include:

- Dataset overview
- Number of rows and columns
- Data types
- Missing value assessment
- Summary statistics
- Distribution of important variables
- Identification of trends and patterns

Use appropriate visualizations such as:

- Bar charts
- Pie charts
- Histograms
- Line charts
- Correlation heatmaps
- Box plots


Step 3: Identify Key Drivers

Determine the factors that most influence the business outcome.

Examples:

Bank Marketing Dataset

- Age
- Job type
- Education level
- Previous campaign outcomes
- Contact methods

Telco Customer Churn Dataset

- Contract type
- Monthly charges
- Tenure
- Internet service
- Payment method

Support your findings with charts, statistics, and clear explanations.


Step 4: Generate Business Insights

Answer questions such as:

- What patterns did you discover?
- What customer behaviors stand out?
- Which customer segments require attention?
- What risks or opportunities exist?

Focus on insights that a business manager can easily understand and act upon.


Step 5: Provide Actionable Recommendations

Based on your findings, provide at least 3–5 recommendations.

Examples:

- Improve customer retention strategies.
- Target specific customer segments.
- Adjust marketing campaigns.
- Introduce loyalty programs.
- Improve customer support services.

Recommendations should be practical and directly supported by your analysis.


Deliverables

Prepare a report containing:

- Key Findings
- Business Insights
- Recommendations
