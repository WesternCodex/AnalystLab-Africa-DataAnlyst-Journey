Task 2 — Define Key KPIs
KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) are the most important metrics in a dashboard.

Examples of KPIs
For Superstore Dataset
Total Revenue
Total Profit
Total Orders
Profit Margin (15%, means a copy keeps $0.15 of every sales as profit after all expenses = (profit/revenue) *100)
Sales Growth
Top Performing Region
Best Selling Category

For COVID-19 Dataset
Total Confirmed Cases
Total Deaths
Recovery Rate
Daily Growth Rate
Cases by Country
Death Rate Trend

Task 4 — Build an Interactive Dashboard

Your dashboard should allow users to interact with the data.
Add These Features
Filters
Slicers
Drill-down capability
Date selection
Region/category filtering
Example:
Click a region to see its sales breakdown
Filter dashboard by year or country

Task 5 — Apply Data Storytelling
Do not only create charts — explain the meaning behind them.

Your dashboard should answer questions such as:
- Which region generates the most profit?
- Which products are underperforming?
- Which country had the highest COVID-19 growth?
- What trends can decision-makers learn from?

Dashboard Design Tips
Make your dashboard:
Clean
Organized
Easy to read
Professional
Consistent in colors and fonts

Avoid:
Too many visuals
Cluttered layouts
Unnecessary charts
